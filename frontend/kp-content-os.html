How it supports the team

From account performance to a usable content brief

The workspace is designed for controlled internal use. It combines approved clinic service context with available metrics from Klinik Putrijaya's own authorized TikTok account.

01

Connect read-only

An authorized administrator connects the Klinik Putrijaya TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization flow. The connection does not request posting permissions.

02

Learn from real signals

The team can review supported profile and public-video metrics from its own account, with source links and timestamps kept visible.

03

Plan the next video

A user starts idea generation for a selected clinic service and receives practical hooks, scripts, shot lists, captions and calls to action for human review.

Clear boundaries

Useful content intelligence without unsafe promises

No automatic postingThe integration reads supported account data and does not publish, edit or delete TikTok content.
No guarantee of viralityPerformance-based ideas are recommendations, not a promise of views, reach, followers or FYP placement.
No patient dataPatient records, consultation details and identifiable medical information must never be entered into the workspace.
Human and medical reviewAuthorized staff review every output, and a qualified doctor must approve medical facts and claims before publication.
